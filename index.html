Evaluation Framework

The 4 Pillars of Prompt Assessment

Our NLP system deconstructs prompts into multi-dimensional rubrics, assessing linguistic intent, structural anchors, and constraints.

1. Structure

Evaluates organizational taxonomy, role definitions, and delimitation of input data.

  • Explicit Role Persona (e.g. "Act as a...")
  • Markdown headers & XML delimiters
  • Sequential step-by-step directives

2. Clarity

Detects lexical ambiguity, contradictory phrasing, and instruction conciseness.

  • Direct imperative verbs
  • Elimination of vagueness / fluff
  • Predictable tone & audience level

3. Context

Validates sufficient background knowledge, domain constraints, and example injection.

  • Target audience specification
  • Few-shot exemplar demonstrations
  • Domain vocabulary anchors

4. Completeness

Ensures safety boundaries, output schema adherence, and edge-case prevention.

  • Strict format schemas (JSON/Markdown)
  • Length & boundary restrictions
  • Negative constraints (what NOT to do)
